## Project Name

**CHER — Coordinated Human Emergency & Response**

## Goal / Problem Statement

An SOS can notify people that an emergency has occurred, but notification alone does not guarantee that someone will take responsibility and provide help. CHER is a Wear OS-based closed-loop emergency response system that detects and verifies emergencies, coordinates nearby human responders, escalates to emergency contacts and emergency services when required, manages backup and handoff, and continues until help is confirmed.
